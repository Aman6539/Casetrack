Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LexTrack ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Advocate Case Management application at aman6539.github.io.

1. Information We Collect

LexTrack collects the following information:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture for authentication purposes only.
  • Case Data: Case titles, case numbers, CNR numbers, client names, court names, hearing dates, and notes that you enter into the app.
  • Google Calendar Data: If you grant calendar permission, we create and update calendar events for your hearing dates. We only create, read, update, and delete events that LexTrack itself has created — we do not access any other calendar events.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you securely via Google Sign-In
  • To store and sync your case data across your devices using Firebase Firestore
  • To automatically create Google Calendar events for court hearing dates
  • To send reminder notifications for upcoming hearings

3. Google Calendar Access

LexTrack requests access to your Google Calendar to automatically create and update hearing date events. Specifically:

  • We use the calendar.events scope
  • We only create events for court hearing dates you have entered in LexTrack
  • We do not read, access, or share any existing calendar events
  • You can revoke calendar access at any time from your Google Account Settings

4. Data Storage

  • Case data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore, a secure cloud database
  • Data is stored under your unique Google account ID — no other user can access your data
  • A local backup copy is kept on your device for offline access

5. Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell your data to any third party
  • Share your case information with anyone
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Access your data except as needed to provide the service

6. Data Security

All data is protected by:

  • Google Firebase security rules — only you can access your own data
  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Google OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all your case data within the app
  • Delete: Delete any or all of your cases from the app
  • Export: Export your data using the Export button in the app
  • Revoke access: Remove LexTrack's access to your Google account at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions

8. Children's Privacy

LexTrack is designed for use by legal professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
